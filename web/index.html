🧠 OceanEmbed-Net AI Architecture

Convolutional representation learning with spatial multi-head self-attention at the bottleneck:

Stage 1
Multi-Scale Conv Encoder (GroupNorm + GELU) Compresses 11-channel surface inputs across 3 hierarchical blocks ($64\times32^2 \to 128\times16^2 \to 256\times8^2$).
Stage 2
Bottleneck Spatial Self-Attention (8×8 Tokens) 64 tokens with 4 attention heads capturing non-local teleconnections, cross-basin Rossby waves, and eddy tilts.
Stage 3
3D Depth Profile Decoder Reconstructs spatial resolution via skip connections and projects directly onto 15 continuous depth temperature channels.
Latent Deliverable: 256 × 8 × 8 Satellite Embeddings (exportable for marine heatwaves & fisheries)
