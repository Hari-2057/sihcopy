Multi-mission satellite remote sensing inputs harmonized onto a canonical 0.25° spatial grid across the North Indian Ocean:

NOAA OISST v2.1 (AVHRR) Sea Surface Temperature (°C) • 0.25° Daily • Upper thermodynamic boundary

SSS
CMEMS Multi-Observation Sea Surface Salinity (PSU) • Freshwater capping & barrier layer stability
Currents
CMEMS Geostrophic + Ekman ($u_o, v_o$) Surface velocity vectors (m/s) • Upper-layer mass and heat transport
Winds
CMEMS / ERA5 ($u_{10}, v_{10}$) 10m Wind stress vectors (m/s) • Ekman pumping & coastal upwelling force

Spatiotemporal Auxiliary Channels Day-of-year $(\sin, \cos)$ cyclic embeddings, latitude gradient & land-sea mask

MODULE 03

🧠 OceanEmbed-Net AI Architecture

Convolutional representation learning with spatial multi-head self-attention at the bottleneck:

Stage 1
Multi-Scale Conv Encoder (GroupNorm + GELU) Compresses 11-channel surface inputs across 3 hierarchical blocks ($64\times32^2 \to 128\times16^2 \to 256\times8^2$).
Stage 2
Bottleneck Spatial Self-Attention (8×8 Tokens) 64 tokens with 4 attention heads capturing non-local teleconnections, cross-basin Rossby waves, and eddy tilts.
Stage 3
3D Depth Profile Decoder Reconstructs spatial resolution via skip connections and projects directly onto 15 continuous depth temperature channels.
Latent Deliverable: 256 × 8 × 8 Satellite Embeddings (exportable for marine heatwaves & fisheries)

Subsurface Marine Heatwave (MHW) Tracking

Marine heatwaves often extend hundreds of meters below the surface, devastating coral reefs and driving cyclone intensification. OceanEmbed detects subsurface thermal anomalies (ΔT > +1.0°C) from surface satellite embeddings with an F1 score of 0.8319.

Arabian Sea Upwelling Suppression

Accurately flags anomalously warm surface and thermocline water when southwest monsoon upwelling weakens along the Oman and Gujarat coasts.

Bay of Bengal Barrier Layer Heat Trapping

Detects heat trapped beneath the freshwater river plume lid (salinity barrier layer) before tropical cyclone genesis.

Equatorial Kelvin Wave Thermal Spikes

Tracks subsurface downwelling Kelvin waves that depress the thermocline and elevate temperatures by 2–3°C across 50–150m.
